# OCR Quality Checklist

- [ ] Source and extracted text were both available for comparison.
- [ ] Headings, paragraphs, and table structure were preserved.
- [ ] Similar-looking letters and numbers were checked.
- [ ] Dates, amounts, percentages, decimal separators, and symbols were checked.
- [ ] Missing or unreadable text was flagged, not guessed.
- [ ] Corrected fields were rechecked against the source.
- [ ] Review log entries are complete and traceable.
- [ ] No confidential source material is included in the public repository.
